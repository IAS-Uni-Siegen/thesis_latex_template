\chapter{Introduction}
\chaptershorttitle{Introduction}
\label{cha:introduction}

The introduction should provide a clear overview of the research topic, the motivation for the study, the research objectives, and the research questions. Central assumptions and limitations of the study should be outlined.

Often the introduction also includes a paragraph on the structure of the thesis. This is optional and depends on the author's preferences. However, because such an overview is mostly redundant with the information provided in the table of contents, it is recommended to omit it for the sake of brevity.
